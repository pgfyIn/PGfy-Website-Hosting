Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. It is PGfy's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you.

Last updated: February 21, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to PGfy's Privacy Policy. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information about you when you use our services and website.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Government-issued identification for KYC verification
  • Payment information
  • Profile information for your PGfy account

Automatically Collected Information

When you use our services, we automatically collect:

  • Device information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (features accessed, time spent on platform)
  • Location information (with your consent)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and improving our services
  • Processing payments and transactions
  • Verifying your identity through KYC
  • Communicating with you about our services
  • Sending important updates and notifications
  • Analyzing and improving our platform's performance

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes:

  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Regular security assessments
  • Secure data storage practices
  • Limited access to personal information by employees

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist in our operations
  • Government authorities when required by law
  • Payment processors for transaction processing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Object to certain data processing
