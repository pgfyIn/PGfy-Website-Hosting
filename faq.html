Frequently Asked Questions

Check out our FAQs to learn more about how PGfy can elevate your experience.

What is PGfy?

PGfy is a platform that helps property owners manage PGs and hostels digitally while helping tenants discover suitable accommodations through the PGfy mobile app.

How do I find a PG using PGfy?

Download the PGfy mobile app, search for accommodations in your preferred city, and explore available options based on your requirements.

Is PGfy free for tenants?

Yes. Tenants can browse and explore available properties through the PGfy app entirely for free.

Can I contact the property owner directly?

Yes. Depending on the property settings, you can connect with the owner or manager for inquiries regarding availability, pricing, and amenities.

What documents are required for booking?

Requirements vary by property, but commonly requested documents include:

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Passport-size Photograph
  • College or Company ID
  • Local Guardian Details (if required)
Can I book accommodation before visiting?

This depends on the individual property's policies. Some properties may allow advance reservations subject to verification.

How do I pay rent?

Many PGfy-enabled properties support digital rent collection through UPI, bank transfer, and other online payment methods.

Is my information secure?

PGfy follows industry-standard security practices to help protect user information and digital records.
