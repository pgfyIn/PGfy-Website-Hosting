How Management Works

Ditch the ledger books and manual coordination. Follow our step-by-step digital process to run your PG business efficiently from anywhere.

Step 1

Register Your PG

Create your property profile by entering essential details like property name, location details, category (PG/Hostels), and target gender preference (Boys/Girls/Unisex).

Scroll down
Step 2

Set Up Rooms & Beds

Configure your room sharing choices (Single, Double, Triple sharing) with pricing details and custom monthly rent or deposit charges to keep listing availability updated.

Step 3

Add Amenities & Rules

Enable property facilities such as high-speed Wi-Fi, daily meals, air conditioning, and define rules (e.g., gate timings, guest policies) to keep your tenants well-informed.

Scroll down
Step 4

Onboard Tenants (KYC)

Manage tenant lifecycle onboarding digitally. Collect documentation, review Aadhaar verification or selfie checks, and sign legally binding digital rent agreements instantly.

Scroll down
Step 5

Track Collections & Grow

Track monthly collections, occupancy percentages, and pending rent details. Send payment reminders with automated payment links directly through the app to maintain healthy cash flow.
