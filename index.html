About PGfy

PGfy is a property management platform built for PGs, hostels, student housing, and co-living operators. We help property owners streamline tenant onboarding, digital KYC, room allocation, rent collection, occupancy tracking, and day-to-day operations through a single platform

Our mission is to simplify accommodation management with technology while creating a seamless experience for both property managers and residents.
