Important Information

Moving and transportation services may be provided by PGfy or by third-party service partners, depending on availability and location.

The final price may depend on: Distance, Volume of goods, Type of vehicle, Packing requirements, Floor/lift availability, Loading and unloading, Special handling, Two-wheeler/car transportation, Date and time, and Other requirements.

The final quotation and service availability will be confirmed before the move.
