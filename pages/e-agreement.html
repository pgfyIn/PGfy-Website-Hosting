What is an e-Agreement?

An e-Agreement is an agreement created, processed and executed electronically instead of relying entirely on physical paperwork.

For PG and rental accommodation, an e-Agreement can contain the terms agreed between the tenant and property owner/property manager, including:

  • Tenant information
  • Property details
  • Room/bed details
  • Rent
  • Security deposit
  • Agreement period
  • Notice period
  • Payment terms
  • Utility charges
  • House rules
  • Move-in and move-out conditions
  • Other applicable terms

Depending on the agreement and applicable law, the document can then go through the required stamping, signing and registration processes.

e-Agreement vs e-Stamp vs eSign

These terms are often confused.

Term What it means
e-Agreement The overall electronic agreement/document workflow
e-Stamping Electronic process for applicable stamp-duty payment
eSign Electronic signing of the document
Registration Separate legal process where registration is required
Notarisation Separate notarial process where applicable

In simple terms:

e-Agreement = The digital document
e-Stamp = Stamp Duty
eSign = Signature
Registration = Separate legal registration
Notarisation = Separate notarial act

One does not automatically replace the others.

Is an e-Agreement Legally Valid?

An electronically created agreement can have legal recognition, but legal validity and enforceability depend on the applicable law, document type, execution process and circumstances.

Depending on the agreement, additional requirements may apply, including:

  • Applicable stamp duty
  • Proper execution
  • Electronic signature requirements
  • Registration
  • Notarisation
  • State-specific requirements
  • Other statutory formalities

Therefore, PGfy does not represent that simply creating an electronic document automatically makes every agreement legally valid, registered or notarised.

Does PGfy Provide e-Notary?

e-Agreement, e-stamping and eSign should not be confused with notarisation.

Where notarisation is legally required, it must be completed through the applicable authorised notarial process.

PGfy's digital agreement workflow can facilitate the electronic documentation journey, while registration and notarisation requirements remain subject to applicable law.
