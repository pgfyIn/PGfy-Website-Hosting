Partner Onboarding Timeline

Get listed and operational on our platform in 8 clear and transparent steps.

Step 1

Register

Create a partner account using your mobile number and email ID on the registration page.

Step 2

Business Information

Add trade names, registration details, GSTIN (optional), and tax identification documents.

Step 3

Identity Verification

Validate owner identity via Aadhaar, PAN card, or standard government-issued credentials.

Step 4

Property Verification

Upload room dimensions, safety certificates, utility records, and coordinates.

Step 5

Accept Partner Terms

Review and digitally sign the partner terms, payout terms, and SLA structure.

Step 6

Property Approval

Our verification team audits documents and publishes your listing live on the app.

Step 7

Receive Tenant Bookings

Tenants browse and request reservations, with notifications sent immediately to you.

Step 8

Receive Settlements

Collected rents and deposits are settled directly into your linked bank accounts.

Verification Requirements

We maintain high levels of listing authenticity by completing four levels of verification checks.

Identity & Business Verification

Owners are verified through national identification logs (Aadhaar/PAN). Corporate partnerships require submission of Registration Certificates, partnership deeds, and GST registration profiles to prevent fraud.

Property Audit & Terms Acceptance

Every listed location undergoes digital property verification, checking address coordinates and safety credentials. Partner Agreement Acceptance is digitally completed before activation of rent collection pipelines.
