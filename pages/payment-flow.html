Deep Dive: Transaction Stages

Understand how we process payments securely behind the scenes.

Step 1

Tenant Initiates Payment

The tenant logs into the booking portal, verifies their monthly invoice or booking deposit, selects a digital payment method, and enters payment credentials.

Step 2

Secure Processing

Transaction details are encrypted and passed via secure API to our payment gateway partner (PayU), which performs verification checks with the issuing bank.

Step 3

Payment Confirmation

Upon successful gateway response, the system updates booking status, sends immediate notifications, and generates tax-compliant digital receipts.

Step 4

Settlement Processing

Net amounts (minus platform subscription fees) are batch-processed and settled securely to the property manager's bank account.

Step 5

Digital Records & Reconciliation

Both tenant and property manager gain access to permanent digital transaction records, invoice history, and monthly reports via their secure dashboards, making taxation and reconciliation effortless.

Important Note

PGfy is a technology platform connecting tenants with verified accommodation providers while facilitating secure payment collection and settlement. All payment flows are regulated under standard gateway policies.
