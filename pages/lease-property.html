What Happens After You Submit?

A quick summary of our process:

  1. Property Enquiry Submitted: You provide your property and leasing requirements.
  2. PGfy Reviews the Information: Our team reviews the location, property type, capacity and leasing requirements.
  3. PGfy Contacts You: We discuss the property, expected lease and preferred operator.
  4. Property Verification: Where applicable, PGfy may conduct its internal Owner & Property Verification process.
  5. Opportunity Matching: Your property can be matched with relevant leasing requirements available through PGfy.
  6. Connect: PGfy can facilitate communication between relevant parties.
  7. Documentation: If the parties proceed, applicable documentation services such as e-Agreement, e-Stamping and eSign may be available through PGfy's ecosystem.
