Careers Faq's

All your questions about joining our team, the interview process, and culture wrapped in one place.

Contact hiring team →
Are you currently hiring for full-time roles?

At present, we are exclusively focusing on expanding our core team through internship roles. We do not have any full-time opportunities available right now, but outstanding interns may be offered full-time roles upon completion of their program.

What does the interview process look like?

Our interview process is streamlined to respect your time while ensuring a mutual fit. It consists of:

  • Step 1: A basic introductory screening call (15 mins).
  • Step 2: An online technical or domain-specific interview (45 mins).
  • Step 3: A final offline interview at our IIIT Bangalore Innovation Centre office to meet the founders.
Do you offer remote work options?

Our current internship roles (Sales & Business Research) require significant hands-on collaboration, so we operate on a hybrid model based out of our Bangalore office. Fully remote positions are not available at this time.

What is the duration of the internships?

Most of our internships run for a duration of 3 to 6 months, giving you enough time to learn the ropes, take ownership of projects, and make a tangible impact on PGfy's growth.

Can I send an open application?

Absolutely! Even if you don't see a role that fits you perfectly right now, we are always looking for passionate talent. Send your resume and a brief intro to hiring@pgfy.in.
