Start & Grow Your PG Business

🏢 Looking to Start or Expand a PG / Hostel? Find. Lease. Set Up. Launch. Manage.

Are you looking to lease an apartment, building or property and start a PG/hostel/co-living business? Or are you an existing property manager looking to expand to a new location? PGfy can help you explore suitable opportunities and connect you with relevant services to get your property operational.

We can assist with:

🏠 Properties Available for Lease — Find suitable buildings, apartments and properties for PG/hostel operations
📍 Location Assessment — Identify locations suitable for PG/hostel demand
🛏️ PG/Hostel Setup Guidance — Plan rooms, beds and accommodation configuration
📋 Property Onboarding — Get your property ready to operate
📄 Agreement & Documentation — e-Agreement, e-Stamping and eSign services where applicable
👥 Tenant Acquisition — List and market your PG through PGfy
💻 PG Management Software — Manage beds, tenants, rent, KYC, complaints and operations
📣 Marketing Support — Promote your property to prospective tenants
🚚 Move-In Services — Packers, movers and tenant move-in assistance
🎁 PGfy Rewards — Access the PGfy partner ecosystem
🔧 Operational Support — Explore additional services required to launch and operate the property
