Frequently Asked Questions

Can I store my belongings temporarily?

Yes. PGfy Storage is intended for temporary as well as longer-duration storage requirements.

Can PGfy pick up my belongings?

Pickup can be requested during the enquiry. Availability depends on the location and service provider.

Can I store only a few boxes?

Yes. You can submit your estimated number of bags, boxes and items, and PGfy can help identify an appropriate storage option.

Can I store furniture?

Furniture and larger belongings can be included in your enquiry. The available storage option will depend on the size and type of items.

Can I store belongings while moving to another city?

Yes. You can provide your current city, destination city and expected storage duration so that the requirement can be assessed.

How long can I store my belongings?

Storage duration can range from short-term requirements to several months or longer, depending on the storage provider.

How much does storage cost?

Pricing can depend on the volume of belongings, storage duration, location, pickup requirements and additional services. Submit your requirement to receive suitable options.
