Payments & Policies

Secure & Convenient Digital Payments

PGfy offers secure and reliable digital payment options for tenants to pay booking amounts, security deposits, monthly rent, and other applicable charges. All payments are processed through trusted payment gateway partners using industry-standard security and encryption.

Accepted Digital Methods

Choose from multiple flexible modes to complete your payments quickly and securely.

UPI

Pay instantly using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or any other UPI app.

Credit Cards

Secure credit card payments with Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, and Diners Club.

Debit Cards

Use your debit card for fast transactions directly from your savings or current account.

Net Banking

Direct payments supported through 50+ leading Indian banks.

Digital Wallets

Quick checkouts with Amazon Pay, Mobikwik, PhonePe, and other wallets.
